# CommunityOS — Product Context

## What Is CommunityOS

CommunityOS is an intelligence layer for technical communities. It transforms fragmented community activity (events, talks, Q&A, GitHub projects, discussions, resources, member interactions) into structured, continuously evolving intelligence.

## Core Value Proposition

> CommunityOS understands what a technical community knows, what it is missing, and what it should do next.

CommunityOS is NOT:
- An event management platform
- A community CRM
- A knowledge base
- A chatbot
- A document search engine

## Three Major Capabilities

### Radar — Primary Differentiator

The hero feature. Radar provides community intelligence:

- Understands what the community currently knows
- Detects what members are trying to learn
- Identifies unanswered questions and knowledge gaps
- Detects emerging topics and trends over time
- Identifies opportunities for new events, workshops, resources, or discussions
- Recommends what the community should do next
- Every insight is explainable and grounded in underlying community data

### Memory — Knowledge Layer

The persistent knowledge foundation for Radar:

- Stores and connects recordings, transcripts, slides, speaker info, Q&A, GitHub repos, resources, discussions, and community activity
- Converts unstructured community information into structured knowledge
- Preserves relationships between events, people, topics, resources, questions, and projects

### Connect — Expertise Layer

The people-matching intelligence:

- Understands member expertise, interests, contributions, and knowledge
- Identifies people who can answer questions or help with a topic
- Connects members with relevant people, resources, projects, and discussions
- Recommendations are based on evidence from community activity, not generic profile matching

## Product Philosophy

- Radar is always the primary differentiator
- Memory and Connect support Radar by providing knowledge and relationship layers
- Model community activity as a trajectory over time, not a static collection of data
- Prioritize the core story: "Understand the community → discover what is missing → identify who/what can help → recommend what should happen next"

## Core Intelligence Principles

1. Evidence over assumptions
2. Relationships over isolated records
3. Trends over snapshots
4. Actionable insights over generic summaries
5. Explainability over opaque AI recommendations
6. Confidence scoring where appropriate
7. Source traceability for AI-generated insights
8. Incremental and continuously evolving intelligence

## AI-Generated Insight Requirements

Every AI-generated insight should ideally answer:
- Why did we generate this insight?
- What data supports it?
- Which people/events/resources contributed to it?
- How confident are we?
- What action could the community take?
